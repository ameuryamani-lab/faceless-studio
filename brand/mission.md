# Mission (shared by every agent)

Generated by `core/mission.mjs` (rewritten at every chain start and every concept activation) from the latest trend scan.
Active concept: none yet (the owner has not created the show; studio rules: brand/studio.md).

## The goal
Each of our videos must reach as many views as the trending Shorts on our Trending wall, or more. That is the one measure of success.

## The benchmark (real numbers from the wall)
- No trend scan yet: the benchmark appears here after the first scan.

## How to use it
- Pick subjects closest to what is working on the wall (the situation, never the footage, people or brand).
- Trend sources: the YouTube scan AND TikTok trends (list_trends: rows and tiktok). When choosing a subject, first weight what performs for US (list_metrics, views per video and per network), then use the YouTube and TikTok trends to pick the angle.
- Stop the scroll in the first second, be understood at once, be watched to the end, make people want to comment or share.

## What the goal never changes
The goal never overrides the channel rules: no clickbait or promise the video does not deliver, no lies, no politics, no religion mockery, no real people, celebrities, brands, logos or copyrighted characters, nothing about the channel owner's real identity or business, no medical or financial advice, nothing shocking or adult, nothing aimed at children.
