# Tracked channels — what research watches

Owner: `trend_scout` (maintains it automatically and logs every addition or removal as an event; no approval needed: research is fully automatic). Read by: outlier_analyst, trend_scout, teardown_analyst.
Only **animated or faceless** short-form channels in English. No live-action creators, no talking heads.
Research reads public metadata with yt-dlp (titles, views, durations, upload dates, auto-captions). Nothing here is scraped by a paid service.

## Format
```
- <platform> · <channel url or handle> · tier: core | adjacent | aspirational · why: <one line> · added YYYY-MM-DD
```
Tiers: `core` = same format and audience; `adjacent` = animated but a neighbouring topic; `aspirational` = much bigger, same format.
